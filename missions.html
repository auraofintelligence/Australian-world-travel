Foreign missions and consular posts in Australia

This directory is built from the current Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade Protocol public directories: Missions in Australia and Consulates in Australia. It includes embassies, high commissions, consulates, consulates-general, other diplomatic posts and honorary posts where DFAT lists them.

Map positions in the connected globe are approximate state-capital references, not office entrances. Entries without a current physical address remain listed here without a map pin. Check the official source before travelling.
